Healthy Futures — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Healthy Futures is a youth soccer and wellness app used by coaches and student athletes in a supervised youth program. Everyone using it is a child or a young person, so we collect as little as possible, never sell any of it, and never use it for advertising.

The short version. We store what you enter in the app so your coach can support you and so your progress carries over between visits. We do not sell data, we do not use it for advertising, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from the Profile tab.

Who is responsible

The Mentors Matter Healthy Futures program operates this app. If you have a question about your data, or you are a parent or guardian who wants data removed, contact your program coach or email saumyasanjeev@tachyonleap.com.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy we need it
Email address, name, and password (stored only as a secure hash — never as readable text) To create your account and let you sign back in

Daily routine check-offs — activity, ball work, stretching, water, fruit and vegetables, breakfast, sleep To show your streak and progress, and to let your coach see who needs support

Messages you send to your coach or to the AI assistant To deliver them, and so the assistant remembers what you talked about earlier
Practice clips you choose to record and send So your coach can review your technique. Only you and your linked coach can view them.

Exercise minutes and sleep hours from your phone's health app — Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android — if you allow it Optional. Fills in the exercise and sleep parts of your day so you don't have to log them by hand. We read only how many minutes of exercise you did, when the longest bout started, and how many hours you slept. We never write anything back to Apple Health or Health Connect, never share this data with anyone outside your own coach's view of your progress, and never use it for advertising. It is stored with your account and deleted when your account is deleted. You can turn access off at any time and the rest of the app keeps working.
Notification token for your device To send reminders. Deleted when you log out or turn notifications off.

We do not collect location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who else sees it

We share data only with the services that make specific features work:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — receives your assistant messages and meal descriptions to generate replies and nutrition scores.
  • Amazon Web Services — hosts the server, and analyses match footage a coach uploads.
  • Expo — delivers reminder notifications to your device.
  • Resend — sends password reset emails.

Your coach sees your routine progress, attendance, level-up criteria, companion scores, messages you send them, and practice clips you send them. Other students never see your data. We do not sell data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Practice clips are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Password reset codes expire after 30 minutes and are single use.
  • Everything else is kept while your account is open, and is deleted when you delete your account.

Children's privacy

This app is only used inside a supervised youth program. Students cannot sign up on their own: an account requires an invite code issued by their coach, so every student is known to a responsible adult before they join. Because the program includes children under 13, a parent or guardian must give consent to the program before a child takes part. That consent is collected by the program itself, offline, and covers the child's use of this app. If you are a parent or guardian and have not given consent, or want to withdraw it, contact your program coach and the account will be removed. Student accounts require a match photo so coaches can identify players in match footage, and a parent or guardian must be named before one can be added. Only a numeric signature is stored, never the photograph. If you would rather your child did not provide one, contact your program coach.

Parents and guardians may ask to see what we hold about their child, correct it, or have it deleted. Contact your program coach or the email address above and we will action it. We do not require a child to give more information than is needed to take part in the program.

Your choices

  • Delete everything. Profile tab → Delete account. This removes your account, routines, check-ins, messages, clips, and any face signature. It cannot be undone.
  • Remove face data. Profile tab → Remove my face data. Because a match photo is required for student accounts, you will be asked to add a new one the next time you open the app.
  • Turn off reminders. Disable notifications for Healthy Futures in your phone settings, or turn nudges off in the app.
  • Turn off health access. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Healthy Futures. On Android: open Health Connect → App permissions → Healthy Futures. Everything else keeps working; the exercise and sleep rows simply stay empty.
  • Skip optional fields. Phone number, allergies, and dietary preference can be left blank.

How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes and are never readable by us.
  • Practice clips are never given a public link — every playback re-checks that the viewer is the student or their linked coach.
  • Face enrolment stores a numeric signature only. The photo is never saved, and the signature is only ever compared against footage uploaded by that student's own coach.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, we will update the date at the top and notify users in the app.
