Healthy Futures — Support

Help for student athletes, coaches, parents and guardians.

Need help? Email saumyasanjeev@tachyonleap.com and we'll reply within two working days. If you are a student, your coach can usually help fastest.

Getting started

  • Students need an invite code from their coach to sign up. Ask your coach for their six-character code.
  • Coaches can sign up without a code. Your invite code appears on the Profile tab once you're in — share it with your athletes.
  • Healthy Futures is used by student athletes inside a supervised youth program. A parent or guardian gives consent to the program before a child takes part.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

On the login screen tap Forgot your password? and we'll email you a six-digit code. The code lasts 30 minutes and works once. If no email arrives, check your spam folder or ask your coach.

I didn't get my invite code / it isn't working.

Codes are six characters and case-insensitive. Ask your coach to read it from their Profile tab. A code only links you to that coach — it isn't a password.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check that notifications are enabled for Healthy Futures in your phone's Settings. Reminders are capped at ten per day, are never sent during a training session, and are quiet overnight.

My coach can't see my practice clip.

Clips only go to the coach you're linked to. Make sure the upload finished — you'll see "Sent to your coach". Clips must be MP4 or MOV and under 200 MB, and are deleted automatically after 90 days.

The AI assistant isn't answering.

The assistant needs an internet connection. If it stays quiet, close and reopen the app. Your conversation history is saved, so nothing is lost.

How do I delete my account?

Profile tab → Delete account. You'll confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account, routines, check-ins, messages, practice clips and any face data. It cannot be undone.

How do I remove just my face data?

Profile tab → Remove my face data. Face matching is optional and off unless you turned it on.

For parents and guardians

You can ask to see what we hold about your child, correct it, or have it deleted. Email saumyasanjeev@tachyonleap.com or speak to your program coach. Our privacy policy explains exactly what is collected and why.

Face matching, which helps a coach identify players in match footage, is off by default and requires a guardian's name before it can be enabled. Only a numeric signature is stored — never the photograph.

Reporting a problem

When emailing us, it helps to include: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and whether you're a coach or a student. Please don't include your password.
